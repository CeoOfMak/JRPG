How to Play ▾
  • Two players alternate placing X and O at grid intersection points.
  • Pieces connect in all 8 directions (horizontal, vertical, and diagonal).
  • To conquer: completely surround an opponent's group — no empty intersections left in any of the 8 directions around the group.
  • Conquered pieces are removed and added to your score as conquered walls.
  • A wall once built cannot be escaped — surrounded pieces are always captured.
  • You cannot play a move that kills your own group without capturing the opponent first (no suicide).
  • Two consecutive passes end the game. Most conquests wins!
  • To conquer an opponent's wall formation, build a bigger wall around their entire connected group.
